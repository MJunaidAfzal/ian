Meet Ian

Founder at 18. 25 years building brands, businesses & ideas.

Ian Silverstein started Creative Action when he was 18 and grew it for 20 years into an award-winning international design consultancy — launching Vodafone in Bahrain and Kuwait and opening offices in Qatar, Oman and Bahrain — before selling the business in 2017.

Through his experience in the Middle East he founded The Qatar Consultancy, specifically to support UK businesses looking to do business in Qatar. Today he uses his experience, knowledge, contacts and enthusiasm to support others to build their businesses, brands and ideas.
